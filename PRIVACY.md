# SwaadNama Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 1 November 2026_

SwaadNama is a Reddit app that lets members of a subreddit rate the food
they've posted about and see the community's favourite places. This policy
explains what the app stores, what it sends outside Reddit, and how to have
your data removed.

SwaadNama runs on Reddit's Developer Platform. Your use of Reddit itself is
covered by [Reddit's Privacy Policy](https://www.reddit.com/policies/privacy-policy).

## What we store

All of this is kept in Reddit's storage for the app, separately for each
subreddit the app is installed in:

- **Your ratings:** your Reddit username, the post you rated (with its
  photos and date), the dish, the place, your rating, and anything optional
  you add: a review, budget, tags (vibes) and the place's location
  (address and map coordinates).
- **Your preferences:** your chosen colour theme and light or dark mode.
- **Usage counts:** which days you opened the app, the day you first used
  it, and how many ratings you've made. These power the "Recently rated" and
  "Top raters" lists and simple usage statistics for the app's developer.
  Daily records are kept for about 40 days.

Your ratings, username and avatar are visible to other members of the
subreddit inside the app.

We don't collect your email address, real name, precise device location,
or any data from outside the subreddit.

## What we send outside Reddit

The app uses two outside services, contacted by the app's server rather
than your device:

- **Photon** (photon.komoot.io), an OpenStreetMap search: when you search
  for a place, the text you type is sent to find matching places. When you
  pick a spot on the map, its coordinates are sent to look up the address.
- **CARTO** (basemaps.cartocdn.com): map images are loaded for the area
  you're viewing.

Your Reddit username and other personal details are never sent to these
services.

## Analytics

The app uses Reddit's Devvit Journeys to record anonymous usage events, for
example when someone starts and finishes adding ratings. These include no
usernames or personal details.

## Removing your data

- You can edit or delete any of your ratings in the app at any time.
  Deleting a rating removes it from the app; your original Reddit post is
  not affected.
- To have all your SwaadNama data removed, send a message to
  [u/thelipaguss](https://www.reddit.com/message/compose/?to=thelipaguss)
  or to the subreddit's moderators.

## Children

SwaadNama follows Reddit's age requirements and is not meant for anyone
too young to use Reddit.
